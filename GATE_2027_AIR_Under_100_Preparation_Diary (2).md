# GATE 2027 Preparation Diary — AIR Under 100 Target

> **Goal:** Prepare systematically for GATE 2027 CSE + DA, aiming for AIR under 100.  
> **Important:** No checklist can guarantee a rank. This system helps track the preparation habits and performance measures that are under your control.

---

## 1. First Page: My Target and Rules

### My target
- [ ] Complete the planned syllabus
- [ ] Understand concepts deeply
- [ ] Solve topic-wise questions
- [ ] Complete and analyze PYQs
- [ ] Revise topics repeatedly
- [ ] Take topic, subject, and full-length tests
- [ ] Reduce repeated mistakes
- [ ] Improve accuracy and test performance

### My core rule

**Measure preparation by:**

`Concepts → Questions → PYQs → Revision → Tests → Accuracy`

Do not measure preparation by study hours alone.

## 10. How to Analyze a Mock Test

After each test, classify every question:

- **A — Correct and confident**
- **B — Correct but guessed/uncertain**
- **C — Wrong despite knowing some relevant concepts**
- **D — Wrong because the concept was not known**
- **E — Unattempted due to time or strategy**

Then record:

- [ ] Review every wrong answer
- [ ] Review correct guesses
- [ ] Identify time-consuming questions
- [ ] Identify avoidable calculation/misreading errors
- [ ] Revise concepts behind mistakes
- [ ] Reattempt selected questions without looking at solutions
- [ ] Set a date for the next check

Prioritize reducing avoidable errors and improving question selection, not merely increasing the number of tests.

## 11. Weekly Study Structure

### Monday–Saturday
For the main topic:
1. Theory and concept understanding
2. Closed-notes recall
3. Basic/standard questions
4. Topic-wise PYQs
5. Error analysis
6. Short revision of older topics

Keep a smaller secondary task only if it does not undermine the main subject.

### Sunday
- [ ] Weekly revision
- [ ] Pending PYQs or reattempts
- [ ] Topic/subject test
- [ ] Test analysis
- [ ] Mistake notebook review
- [ ] Formula revision
- [ ] Plan the next week

If you need to study a new topic on Sunday to stay on track, do so—but protect time for revision and analysis.

## 12. What to Do

- [ ] Follow one primary source/teacher per subject unless there is a clear reason to change.
- [ ] Start PYQs while studying the subject; do not postpone all PYQs until the end.
- [ ] Attempt questions before checking solutions.
- [ ] After reading a solution, close it and reproduce the reasoning yourself.
- [ ] Revise older topics every week.
- [ ] Analyze tests, including correct guesses.
- [ ] Track accuracy, retention, and error patterns.
- [ ] Keep a realistic plan with buffer time.
- [ ] Maintain adequate sleep and regular breaks.
- [ ] Use the official syllabus and exam information to guide coverage.

## 13. What Not to Do

- [ ] Do not rewrite the timetable every few days instead of following it.
- [ ] Do not switch teachers/resources repeatedly without a specific reason.
- [ ] Do not count lecture hours as proof of mastery.
- [ ] Do not copy a solution immediately without first attempting the problem.
- [ ] Do not mark a topic complete after one reading.
- [ ] Do not ignore old topics once the lecture is finished.
- [ ] Do not take mocks without analyzing them.
- [ ] Do not compare progress based only on what other students claim.
- [ ] Do not sacrifice sleep consistently to increase study hours.
- [ ] Do not chase a perfect diary; use it to make decisions.

---

## 14. The Final Operating Rule

Every day:

**PLAN → EXECUTE → MEASURE → ANALYZE → CORRECT → REPEAT**

At night, ask:
1. Did I complete the three most important tasks?
2. What can I now solve or recall that I could not do before?
3. What mistake must I avoid repeating?
4. What is the first task tomorrow?

Every Sunday, ask:
1. Is my accuracy improving?
2. Are my old topics still retrievable?
3. Are repeated mistakes decreasing?
4. What do test results say I should prioritize next?

**Remember:** AIR under 100 is an ambitious target, not a guaranteed outcome. Focus on controllable actions and evidence of mastery rather than rank predictions.
